The sustainability score combines the user-defined criterion weights with the mean outperformance probabilities. These values are based on pairwise probabilities calculated as P(A < B) + 0.5P(A = B) and averaged over all feasible opponents; the resulting sustainability score is not a probability of being the global winner.
