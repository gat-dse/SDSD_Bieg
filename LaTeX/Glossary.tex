\chapter*{Glossary}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Glossary}

\begin{description}
    \item[Slab system]
    The load-bearing floor structure, defined by its structural cross-section and structural system.

    \item[Structural cross-section]
    The load-bearing material composition and geometry represented in the structural model.

    \item[Floor system]
    The complete floor, including the slab system and the non-structural floor build-up.

    \item[Floor build-up]
    The non-structural layers placed above the structural cross-section to satisfy the acoustic requirements.

    \item[Global warming potential (GWP)]
    Standardised environmental-impact metric expressed as kg CO$_2$-eq. In this thesis, results are normalised per square metre of floor area.

    \item[Ultimate limit state (ULS)]
    Design state for the verification of structural safety.

    \item[Serviceability limit state deflection (SLS 1)]
    Verification of vertical deformation under service conditions.

    \item[Serviceability limit state vibration (SLS 2)]
    Verification of vibration and user comfort under service conditions.

    \item[Accidental fire design situation (Fire)]
    Design situation for the verification of structural safety under accidental fire exposure.

    \item[Envelope design criterion (ENV)]
    The optimisation criterion used to minimise GWP while satisfying ULS, SLS 1, SLS 2 and Fire simultaneously.

    \item[Governing design criterion]
    The design criterion that controls the required dimensions of the optimised structural cross-section.

    \item[Plotting envelope]
    The area bounded by the minimum and maximum calculated result at each span for the plotted metric.

    \item[Environmental Product Declaration (EPD)]
    A standardised declaration of product-specific environmental impacts.

    \item[Uncertainty quantification (UQ)]
    The analysis of how uncertainty in the input data affects the calculated results and the comparison between slab systems.

    \item[Timber--concrete composite (TCC)]
    A structural system in which timber and concrete act together through a mechanical connection.

    % explain loads, e.g. g0,k : selfweight structural cross section

\end{description}
